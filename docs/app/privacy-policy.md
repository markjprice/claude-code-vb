\# HabitTrail Privacy Policy



\_Last updated: \[DATE]\_



HabitTrail is designed to work entirely on your own phone. This page explains exactly what that means for your data.



\## What HabitTrail collects



HabitTrail does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data to us or to any third party. There are no user accounts, no sign-in, and no server that HabitTrail communicates with.



Everything HabitTrail stores — your habits, their names, reminder times, and completion history — is saved locally on your device using your phone's own storage. This data never leaves your phone unless you choose to share it yourself, such as using the app's built-in share feature to send a progress message to someone.



\## Notifications



If you set a daily reminder for a habit, HabitTrail asks your phone's operating system for permission to send local notifications. These notifications are scheduled and delivered entirely by your phone. No reminder data is sent to us or to any external service.



You can review or revoke this permission at any time in your phone's system settings.



\## Sharing



HabitTrail includes an optional feature to share a short progress message using your phone's standard share sheet — the same one used by other apps on your phone. HabitTrail does not know or control where you choose to send that message; that's entirely up to you and the app or contact you select.



\## Data you control



Because everything is stored only on your device:



\- Deleting a habit removes it, and its history, from your phone.

\- Uninstalling HabitTrail removes all of its stored data along with it.

\- There is no account to delete and no server-side copy of your data to request or remove, because none exists.



\## Children's privacy



HabitTrail does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children, because it does not collect data at all.



\## Changes to this policy



If HabitTrail's data practices ever change — for example, if a future version adds an optional account or sync feature — this page will be updated to describe exactly what changed and why, before that update reaches you.
