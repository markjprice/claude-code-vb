\# HabitTrail Support



Need help with HabitTrail? Start here.



\## Frequently asked questions



\*\*How do I add a habit?\*\*

Tap the add button on the Habits screen, enter a name, and optionally set a daily reminder time. Save to add it to your list.



\*\*How do I mark a habit done?\*\*

Tap the habit's done indicator on the Habits screen. Tap it again to undo, if you marked it by mistake.



\*\*How do reminders work?\*\*

If you set a reminder time for a habit, HabitTrail asks permission to send you a daily local notification at that time. You can change or clear a habit's reminder anytime from its detail screen.



\*\*I'm not getting reminders. What should I check?\*\*

\- Confirm notification permission is enabled for HabitTrail in your phone's system settings.

\- Confirm the habit still has a reminder time set — editing a habit doesn't always keep a previous reminder.

\- Make sure your phone isn't in a mode that blocks notifications, such as Do Not Disturb, during the reminder time.



\*\*How do I edit or delete a habit?\*\*

Tap the habit from the Habits screen to open its detail screen. From there, you can edit its name or reminder time, or delete it. Deleting asks for confirmation first, since it can't be undone.



\*\*Will I lose my habits if I close the app?\*\*

No. HabitTrail saves your habits on your phone automatically, so they're still there the next time you open the app.



\*\*Does HabitTrail work without an internet connection?\*\*

Yes. HabitTrail is designed to work fully offline. Nothing about adding, tracking, or reminding you about a habit requires an internet connection.



\*\*Does HabitTrail sync between my devices?\*\*

Not currently. Habits are stored only on the device where you added them.



\*\*How do I share my progress?\*\*

From a habit's detail screen, use the share option to send a short progress message through your phone's standard share feature — text, email, or any app you choose.



\*\*What data does HabitTrail collect?\*\*

None beyond what's stored on your own device. See the \[Privacy Policy](privacy-policy.md) for details.
